Allow screen recording?

While a recording is on, this extension takes a screenshot of your browser tab each time you click, type in a field, copy, paste or switch tabs. There's no video.

  • Password and card-number fields are covered in every screenshot.
  • You can pause at any time, and nothing is captured while paused.
  • Nothing leaves your computer until you review the steps and press Send to Claude.
  • Sent recordings go to the Admin Hub, where you and Miguel can see them.

A 5-second countdown starts, then do the task as usual.
